## Full description (max 4000)
Global Challenge Chess is chess for everyone: test yourself against real players from around the world, or practise offline against a strong chess engine.

PLAY ONLINE
• Find an opponent in seconds: 3, 5 or 10 minute games
• Rated games with a live rating (Glicko-2), just like on big chess sites
• Server-side clocks, so time is fair for both players
• Lost connection? Come back to your game automatically
• Offer a draw, resign, or claim the win if your opponent leaves
• No one free right now? Play the bot while you keep searching

PLAY THE BOT
• Stockfish engine runs right on your phone, no internet needed
• Three levels: Beginner, Intermediate and Strong
• Choose white, black or random
• Undo moves and learn from your mistakes

PLAY WITH A FRIEND
• Two players on one phone, the board turns for each side

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
• Large, clear board that fills your screen
• Smooth drag-and-drop or tap-to-move
• Full move list, check, checkmate, stalemate and draw rules
• English, Russian and Uzbek

No sign-up, no ads. Open the app and play.

Global Challenge Chess is open source and uses the Stockfish engine (GPLv3): https://github.com/Max19942008/global-challenge-chess
